01

Multi-CEX perception

Funding rates and open interest pulled from 6 exchanges every 5 min. Cross-CEX avgFR computed as the baseline crowd positioning signal.

02

FR z-score detection

frZ = (current avgFR − 24h mean) ÷ 24h stddev. When frZ ≥ +1.5, the crowd is statistically over-long → SHORT. When frZ ≤ −1.5, crowd is over-short → LONG. Hold in between.

03

OI momentum gate

OI log-momentum is checked before any entry. If open interest is still actively building (+30 bps threshold), the trade is blocked — entering into an accelerating crowd trend defeats the contrarian premise.

04

Adversarial debate

Architect proposes direction. Auditor stress-tests FR bias, regime conditions, and crowd concentration signals from all 6 sources. Arbiter resolves with final confidence score.

05

Regime-calibrated execution

Position size = confidence × sizeMultiplier × regimeFactor × base. Normal regime: full size. Extreme regime (|frZ| ≥ 2σ): ×0.7 caution. Limit orders on Bitget USDT-margined perpetuals, isolated margin.
